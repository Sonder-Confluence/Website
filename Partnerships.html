02 / Proven Ground

Case Studies in Desert Resilience

When diverse minds convene under extreme conditions, the oasis ceases to be a metaphor and becomes functional community infrastructure.

Jordanian Badia 2023–Present

Wadi Rum Solar Cooperative

Bedouin pastoralists joined forces with civil renewable engineers to construct nomadic solar shade structures that shelter goats at midday while powering water chillers and sat-comm units.

“The sun which once dictated retreat now powers our community assemblies after twilight.”
Output: 120kW Decentralized solar_power
Sonoran Basin 2022–Present

Sonoran Seed Vault & Kitchen

Indigenous Tohono O'odham farmers, botanists, and sound artists established an adobe clay repository housing 400 drought-adapted heirloom seeds alongside oral seed songs.

“A seed is an unbroken memory of rain stored in hard casing until kinship awakens it.”
Heirloom Strains: 400+ Preserved potted_plant
Rajasthan Thar 2024–Present

Thar Water Stewards Circle

Reviving century-old subterranean rainwater stepwells (kunds) coupled with decentralized filtration sensor networks managed democratically by women elder councils.

“We do not conquer the sands; we simply remember where the subterranean streams rest.”
