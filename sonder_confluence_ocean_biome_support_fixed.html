Questions & Currents

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my donation tax-deductible? expand_more

Yes. Sonder Confluence operates as an internationally registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit cultural initiative. Official fiscal receipts are automatically issued via email upon pledge confirmation.

Can I change or cancel my tributary support at any time? expand_more

Always. There are zero locked contracts or penalty structures. You can adjust your donation cadence, pause contributions, or cancel instantly with one click from your donor account portal.

How are community workshop leaders selected? expand_more

Facilitators are chosen via localized peer councils within each biome rather than centralized corporate appointments. This guarantees grassroots autonomy and cultural relevance.
